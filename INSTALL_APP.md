# How to Install Solar CRM as an App

This guide explains how to install the Solar CRM Portal as a standalone app on your device for the best experience.

## iPhone (iOS) Installation

1. **Open Safari Browser**
   - Open Safari on your iPhone
   - Navigate to your Solar CRM Portal URL

2. **Access Share Menu**
   - Tap the Share button (square with arrow pointing up) at the bottom of the screen

3. **Add to Home Screen**
   - Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
   - You'll see the app icon and name "Solar CRM"

4. **Confirm Installation**
   - Tap "Add" in the top right corner
   - The app icon will appear on your home screen

5. **Launch the App**
   - Tap the Solar CRM icon on your home screen
   - The app will open in full-screen mode without browser controls

## Android Installation

### Using Chrome:

1. **Open Chrome Browser**
   - Open Chrome on your Android device
   - Navigate to your Solar CRM Portal URL

2. **Install App**
   - Tap the three dots menu (⋮) in the top right
   - Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen"
   - A popup will appear asking to install

3. **Confirm Installation**
   - Tap "Install" in the popup
   - The app will be added to your home screen and app drawer

4. **Launch the App**
   - Find "Solar CRM" in your app drawer or home screen
   - Tap to open the app

### Alternative Method:

1. **Look for Banner**
   - When you first visit the site, you may see a banner at the bottom
   - Tap "Install" on this banner

2. **Or Use Menu**
   - Tap the three dots menu (⋮)
   - Select "Install Solar CRM"

## Benefits of Installing as App

- **Faster Access**: Launch directly from home screen
- **Full Screen**: No browser address bar or navigation buttons
- **Offline Support**: Access cached content when offline
- **App-like Experience**: Looks and feels like a native app
- **Better Performance**: Optimized loading and caching
- **Push Notifications**: Receive important updates (if enabled)

## Troubleshooting

### iPhone Issues:

**"Add to Home Screen" not showing:**
- Make sure you're using Safari (not Chrome or other browsers)
- Check that you're on the actual website page (not a redirect)
- Try refreshing the page

**App not opening in full screen:**
- Delete the app from home screen
- Clear Safari cache (Settings > Safari > Clear History and Website Data)
- Reinstall following the steps above

### Android Issues:

**"Install app" option not showing:**
- Make sure you're using Chrome browser
- Check your internet connection
- Try refreshing the page
- Make sure you're not in Incognito mode

**Installation fails:**
- Check available storage space
- Update Chrome to the latest version
- Clear browser cache and try again

## Need Help?

Contact your system administrator if you encounter any issues during installation.
